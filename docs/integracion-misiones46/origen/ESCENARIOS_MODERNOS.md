# Escenarios modernos de Misión 6

Referencias del usuario: CASA_MODERNA.webp y SALA_MODERNA.webp del Escritorio, incluidas en referencias_documento/. Se usó la herramienta integrada de imágenes con la guía imagegen para conservar el diseño moderno y adaptar el acabado y la paleta al juego.

Recursos vigentes: entrega1-nueva/src/main/resources/cliente/mision6/casa.png y sala.png. La casa no contiene un helicóptero dibujado, cuadrícula ni rótulos: las naves de la llegada las renderiza el juego. La sala conserva entrada, TV/chimenea, sofá, mesa y escalera. Se ajustan el aterrizaje, las posiciones de los personajes y las colisiones al nuevo decorado; las acciones y el flujo de juego se conservan.

El mensaje de Mr. Palomo se refiere a «Juan, el candidato a la alcaldía», durante su campaña. Los fondos anteriores se conservan en referencias_documento/escenarios_anteriores/.

## Prompt de casa

Edit target image 1: CASA_MODERNA, the user's exact modern two-storey campaign house design. Image 2 is ONLY the existing game's rendering/palette reference. Produce a finished wide 16:9 pixel-art game background of THIS SAME modern concrete-and-wood house: garage left, main entrance center, upper balcony with glass rail and plants, flat rooftop helipad, garden and fence, front perspective. KEEP modern architectural identity from image 1; do NOT copy the ornate palace from image 2. Reinterpret in the same crisp cozy game pixel-art and twilight violet, dusk-blue, soft warm gold lighting of image 2; modern gray concrete, warm wooden door, restrained violet accents. Crucial: REMOVE the helicopter completely, leave the rooftop H landing pad EMPTY and unobstructed; do not draw ANY aircraft. REMOVE checkerboard, all labels, annotations, guide lines and words (the H landing mark alone may remain). Replace checkerboard with a coherent twilight city sky and subtle distant city context. Compose the entire house centered, entrance and garden visible, with rooftop pad centered near 51% width and 25% height of the complete canvas, enough clear sky above for an incoming game helicopter. Finished opaque full-bleed landscape game scene, no UI, no characters, no extra buildings in foreground. Preserve the reference house layout, not a mansion or palace.

## Prompt de sala

Edit target image 1: SALA_MODERNA, the user's exact modern living room layout. Image 2 is ONLY the existing game's pixel-art rendering and violet/gold twilight palette reference. Create a finished wide 16:9 full-bleed game background of THIS SAME modern room: entry door on far left, modern TV/fireplace wall left-center, built-in shelves, a wide upholstered sofa with cushions center-right facing camera, coffee table in front, stairway up at right, tall right windows and plants. KEEP the modern residential design, NOT the ornate palace decor of image 2. Reinterpret with the game's cozy detailed crisp pixel-art style, deep violet upholstered sofa, dusky blue/gray walls, warm wood, soft golden modern lamps and twilight through windows; restrained palette matching image 2. REMOVE checkerboard, outer border, guide lines, labels and ALL text. Fill the whole frame with the room, no cutaway/checkerboard margin. Important gameplay composition: enough clear connected walkable floor across the lower 35% of the scene, sofa cushions centered around 55% width and 54% height, coffee table just below; characters will be drawn by the game sitting on the sofa, so leave it EMPTY with no people. No characters, no UI, no text, no aircraft. Preserve the distinctive modern stairway, sofa, entry and fireplace arrangement from image 1.
